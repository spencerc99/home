## 6 · So can we realize the original…
<!-- 2002:158 -->

So can we realize the original promise of perpetual energy? I think I've found that the energy I care about comes in these very human, unoptimized, error-full forms. Rather than a perfect closed circle, I'm interested in a continuing spiral — the kind that requires people to play off each other and shows the life of everyone who has contributed.
Perpetual Play takes the idea of the perpetual motion machine but brings it to a human scale. Rather than something we have to optimize and capture, it can be something free and light. The way we play together, generate new possibilities, turn one encounter into one that changes the course of lives, is a kind of power that cannot be measured. We have so much capability to change the world that we don't recognize, and it all starts from giving ourselves **the permission to play with one another**, and being open to what possibilities can emerge.

## 9 · We're not starting from an easy…
<!-- 2176:43 -->

We're not starting from an easy place. There is not a lot of hope for the internet these days. Dead internet theory has become popular again — there's now more bot activity than human activity on the web. AI-generated slop floods the web and people are scared whether the open web is doomed. 

## 10 · But just because the quantity of…
<!-- 2083:714 -->

But just because the quantity of bots is higher doesn't mean the life or quality of what people experience is worse.
**If dead internet theory posits that the internet will eventually become only bots, alive internet theory proclaims we will never let the open internet die**. We will always find a way to look for each other, to answer a call for help, to share a laugh and an argument right after one another.
To believe in each other, we must believe in the open Internet.

## 14 · playhtml is an open source tool…
<!-- 1:105 -->

playhtml is an open source tool I created in 2023 for bridging the gap between expressive personal websites and new social interfaces and interactions. It makes it easy for anyone who can make a personal website to create a **live shared space** — everything you do happens in real time for everyone else, and the things you do, like turning on a lamp or leaving a note, stay for others to find.

## 15 · poems we found on the internet
